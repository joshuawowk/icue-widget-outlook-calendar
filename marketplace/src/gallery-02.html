Your next days, at a glance

An agenda for the next 1 to 14 days with a live countdown in the header.

Grouped by dayDay separators such as Today and Tomorrow, with locale-aware times.
Next-event countdownThe header shows today's date and the time to your next event.
Busy markerThe countdown turns red during a meeting and the current row is highlighted.
Hide all-day eventsA switch removes all-day events from the agenda.
Offline resilienceKeeps the last good data with an offline tag if the proxy is unreachable.
Made to fitTwo or three columns on large horizontal slots, with your colors and image.
